## Overview
This document provides a 10–15 minute evaluation walkthrough for prospective buyers, software architects, and due-diligence teams evaluating Quantum Terminal.

### Walkthrough Details
1. **Launch Presentation Dashboard**: Upon initial launch, review the 12-module Buyer Presentation Dashboard.
2. **Explore Multi-Chart Canvas**: Test 1x1 to 2x3 chart cell grid switching, timeframe selection (`1m` to `1W`), and custom drawing tools.
3. **Submit Paper Order**: Use the Order Panel or canvas line dragging to place a paper order. Observe instant fill execution, balance adjustment, and line drag handles.
4. **Inspect Portfolio & Risk Lab**: View Equity, Balance, Free Margin, Drawdown alerts, and asset distribution in the Bottom Panel.
5. **Launch Market Replay Studio**: Play back historical ticks step-by-step to test discretionary execution skills.
6. **Open Institutional Options Desk**: Review options chains, Black-Scholes Greeks, implied volatility skew charts, and strategy payoff diagrams.
7. **Test Script Studio**: Write and evaluate custom indicator mathematical expressions.
8. **View Level-2 Order Flow & DOM**: Inspect bid/ask depth visualization, delta panels, and time-and-sales execution log.
9. **Simulate Smart Order Router (SOR)**: Test multi-venue order routing across simulated pools (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, LMAX).
10. **Review AI Analyst**: Evaluate AI Copilot signals and technical pattern heuristic outputs.
11. **Check System Health Panel**: Click **⚡ Health** in the header to view real-time diagnostic statuses across REST API, WebSockets, DB, and AI.
12. **Test Quantum Mobile Pro**: Click **📱 Mobile** or resize browser to test touch canvas panning, vertical price scale dragging, economic event overlays, and bottom navigation.
